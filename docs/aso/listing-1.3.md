# Pockling 1.3: the listing to enter

Written on 14 Nov 2026 for 1.3 (5), the widget update (main 700fea0). It is docs/ASO.md's
approved listing plus what the widget changes. Not in App Store Connect yet: the 1.3 version can
only be made once 1.2 (4) is released. Every field is within App Store Connect's limits (checked
by script: names and subtitles 30, keywords 100, promotional text 170, description and What's New
4000).

## What the widget changes

- **Keywords:** "widget", for "pet widget" and "widget pet" (Pixel Pals, Widgetable and Mochi
  Pets win "virtual pet" and "cute pet" with widgets). It replaces "raise" in both English
  fields and "bebe" in Spanish, and is added to the Hindi field.
- **Promotional text:** says it now lives on your Home Screen. To make room, the English drops
  "stroke it" and "never nags", and the Spanish "dentro de" and "luego".
- **Description:** a new section, ON YOUR HOME SCREEN (EN TU PANTALLA DE INICIO, होम स्क्रीन पर),
  before the last one. The English also has docs/ASO.md's new first line and KIND BY DESIGN line.
- **What's New:** below, for each language. It names only what the widget does (commit f44d914):
  the pet, its name, how it feels, what it needs most and whether it's asleep; small and medium on
  the Home Screen, StandBy, and a ring, a card and the line above the clock on the Lock Screen; a
  tap opens the game; nothing leaves the phone.
- **Screenshots:** in both iPhone sets, a widget shot is 02 and "Hide and seek" goes (made from
  700fea0, in `docs/aso/store/`). The iPad set is unchanged.

**Description (2288):**

```
Pockling is a cute virtual pet for your iPhone: there's a tiny friend waiting behind your screen.

Pick an egg, tap until it cracks, and meet your Pockling: a very small, very fluffy creature with enormous eyes who has decided that you are the best thing that has ever happened.

ALL THE LOVE
Stroke it and it purrs, and you feel the purr in your hand. Boop its nose. Tickle its tummy until it rolls over laughing. Hold it for a cuddle. Pull it and it stretches. It presses its nose on your screen and fogs up the glass. Sometimes it licks it.

FEED IT
Drag a snack up to its mouth and watch it nibble. Every pet has favorites, and one food it can't stand. You'll find out which.

BATH TIME
Soap it into a ball of bubbles, rinse it, then rub it dry until it poofs up twice its size. Squeak the duck. Stay for the rainbow.

WALKIES
Hold your finger and it trots along on a little lead through the park. Berries to find, flowers to sniff, butterflies to chase, muddy puddles it will absolutely jump in, and other Pocklings to say hello to. At night there are fireflies.

PLAY
Flick the ball and it tears off to fetch it. Catch falling snacks in Snack Drop. Play Hide and Seek, where it is very bad at hiding.

IT TALKS BACK
Say something and your pet says it right back in a squeaky little voice, lips moving in time.

GROWS WITH YOU
It lives on the real clock. The sky through its window follows your day, and when it's sleepy you tuck it into bed under a star projector. Look after it and it grows from a baby to a kid to all grown up. Come back each day to fill your stamp book, and spend your berries on 15 outfits, 6 rooms and plenty of treats.

ON YOUR HOME SCREEN
Add the Pockling widget and your pet stays with you on your Home Screen, on your Lock Screen and in StandBy. It shows how your Pockling is feeling and what it needs, and it gets hungry and sleepy on time, just as it does in the app. Tap it to say hello.

SIX KINDS TO HATCH
Kitling, Bunling, Puppling, Cubling, Foxling and Drakling, each in four coats.

KIND BY DESIGN
No ads. No in-app purchases. No accounts. No timers that nag you. Nothing you say leaves your device, and the app's anonymous usage data can be switched off. And your pet can never die or run away: if you've been gone, it's just very glad to see you.
```

**What's New (477):**

```
Your Pockling has a widget now.

Keep your pet on your Home Screen, on your Lock Screen and in StandBy. The widget shows your Pockling and its name, how it's feeling, what it needs most and whether it's fast asleep. It gets hungry on time and wakes up when it's rested, just as it does in the app. Tap it to go straight to your pet.

Small and medium sizes for the Home Screen, and a ring, a card and a line above the clock for the Lock Screen. Nothing in it leaves your phone.
```
