Pockling

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Pockling never asks who you are. There are no accounts, no advertising and no tracking of any kind, and we don't sell or share anything with anyone. The web version sends nothing about you anywhere. The iOS app, from version 1.2, sends a little anonymous usage data to our own server so we can see what Pocklings like doing, and you can turn that off.

What stays on your device

Your pet's name, its needs, its growth, your berries, its outfits and your settings are saved only on your device: in the app's own storage on iPhone and iPad, and in your browser's storage on the web. Removing the app or clearing the browser's data removes them. If you use iCloud or computer backups of your device, the backup includes the app's save.

What the iOS app sends

The app sends these to 9io's games server at 9io.ai, and nothing more:

  • An anonymous install ID. A random code the app makes on your device the first time it runs. It isn't your name, your Apple Account or your device's advertising identifier, and it isn't linked to any of them. Deleting the app forgets it, and a new install gets a new one.
  • Usage events. Short notes such as "the app opened", "a Bunling hatched", "a game of Fetch started", "it finished with a score of 12", "a snack was eaten", "the pet grew up" or "a reminder was opened", with how long the app was open. Each comes with the app's version, the iOS version, the model of device, its language and its time zone. Never your pet's name, never anything you type, and never what you say to your pet.
  • The push token, when notifications are on. If you allow notifications, Apple gives the app a token for sending them to this device. The app sends it to us with whether notifications are allowed and whether you want reminders and news, so we only ever send news to people who asked for it. It sends them again when any of that changes, and at most once a day otherwise.

We use this only to understand and improve our games, and to send the notifications you chose through Apple's push service. We don't share it with anyone else, and never use it for advertising. The server is 9io's own, on Amazon Web Services. Usage events are kept for up to 400 days, and a push token is deleted as soon as Apple says it's no longer in use.

No IP addresses

Every request on the internet comes from an address. Our games server doesn't store the addresses the app's requests come from, and keeps no logs of them.

Turning it off

In the app, open the menu, then Settings, and turn off Share anonymous usage data. The app stops sending usage events and deletes any it hasn't sent yet. If you've allowed notifications, the push token and your notification choices still go to us, with the install ID and the app and device details above, because they decide which notifications reach you. Turn notifications off, in Pockling's Settings or in iOS Settings, and the app tells our server, which then sends you none.

Notifications

  • Reminders. If you turn them on, a hello at 6 pm each evening and a nudge after a few days away are scheduled on your device, with your pet's name in them. The name stays on your device. When you open one, the app counts it as a usage event.
  • News. Only if you choose Reminders and news, or turn on News and new games: now and then, a notification about new games and events from 9io. It's off unless you turn it on.

You can change either at any time in Pockling's Settings, or turn off all of Pockling's notifications in iOS Settings.

The microphone

Pockling only listens while you have talk-back turned on, and asks your permission first. When you speak, the sound is held in memory just long enough for your pet to say it back in a squeaky voice, then it is thrown away. It is never written to storage and never sent anywhere. You can turn talk-back off at any time, and refuse or withdraw the permission in your device's settings; everything else in the game still works.

Other device features

  • Tilt. The app reads the angle of your device to make the room shift gently as you move it. Nothing is stored.
  • Pet cards. The photo card is made on your device. It only goes somewhere if you choose to share it, using your device's own share sheet.

The web version

The web version sends nothing about you: no install ID, no usage data and no notifications. It's served by our web server, which keeps ordinary request logs (such as IP address and page requested) to keep the site running and secure. We don't use them to identify anyone, and we don't use cookies, analytics or advertising here.

Children

Pockling is suitable for all ages. It never asks who anyone is, and its usage data is anonymous and the same for everyone, children included.
